Job Title: Senior Data Scientist - AI & NLP

Responsibilities:
- Build and deploy Machine Learning and NLP models using Python, scikit-learn, PyTorch, or TensorFlow.
- Design scalable data pipelines and perform exploratory data analysis using Pandas, NumPy, and SQL.
- Develop vector embeddings and semantic search pipelines using Sentence-Transformers and LLM frameworks.
- Collaborate with cross-functional software teams to integrate APIs (FastAPI/Flask) into cloud infrastructure (AWS, Docker).

Required Qualifications:
- Strong expertise in Python, SQL, Machine Learning, and Natural Language Processing (NLP).
- Hands-on experience with spaCy, Transformers, Hugging Face, and Vector Databases (FAISS/ChromaDB).
- Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Data Science, or related field.
